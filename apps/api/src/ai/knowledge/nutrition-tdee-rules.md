# Nutrition & TDEE Scientific Rules

## 1. Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) & TDEE Calculations
Energy expenditure is estimated using the **Mifflin-St Jeor** equation:
- **Men**: `BMR = 10 * weight(kg) + 6.25 * height(cm) - 5 * age(years) + 5`
- **Women**: `BMR = 10 * weight(kg) + 6.25 * height(cm) - 5 * age(years) - 161`

### Lifestyle Multipliers
- **Sedentary (1.20)**: Desk job, little to no structured physical exercise.
- **Lightly Active (1.375)**: Light training 1-3 days/week or 6,000-8,000 steps/day.
- **Moderately Active (1.55)**: Moderate training 3-5 days/week or 8,000-11,000 steps/day.
- **Very Active (1.725)**: Heavy resistance training 6-7 days/week or athletic labor.
- **Extremely Active (1.90)**: Two-a-day training sessions or elite competitive athletics.

`TDEE = BMR * Lifestyle Multiplier`

## 2. Safe Caloric Deficits and Hard Safety Floors
- **Target Rate of Loss**: 0.5% to 1.0% of total body weight per week. Rates exceeding 1.0%/week correlate with excessive lean mass catabolism and hormonal suppression.
- **Strict Clinical Caloric Floors**:
  - **Biological Men**: Minimum **1500 kcal/day**. Under no circumstance should plans prescribe < 1500 kcal without direct medical supervision.
  - **Biological Women**: Minimum **1200 kcal/day**. Prescriptions < 1200 kcal/day trigger hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis dysfunction and metabolic slowdown.
- **Low BMI Safeguard**: If `BMI < 18.5`, caloric deficits are strictly prohibited; energy surplus (+200 to +400 kcal above maintenance) is mandatory.

## 3. Macronutrient Distribution Benchmarks
- **Protein**:
  - Hypertrophy / Maintenance: **1.6g to 2.2g / kg** of total body mass.
  - Hypocaloric Deficit (Cutting): **2.0g to 2.6g / kg** of fat-free mass (or 2.2g/kg total mass) to prevent muscle protein breakdown.
- **Dietary Fat**:
  - Minimum floor: **0.6g to 0.8g / kg** (or 20-25% of total caloric intake) to preserve steroid hormone synthesis and fat-soluble vitamin absorption.
- **Carbohydrates**:
  - Remaining daily caloric allocation after protein and fat are satisfied. Serves as primary substrate for anaerobic glycolytic resistance training performance.

## 4. Refeed Protocols & Diet Breaks
- **Structured Refeeds**: For individuals below 12% body fat (men) or 20% body fat (women) in prolonged caloric restriction (> 4 weeks), prescribe 1 to 2 consecutive days at **maintenance calories**, with the surplus coming predominantly from starchy carbohydrates (reducing fat to 15-20%).
- **Diet Breaks**: 1 full week at maintenance calories every 8-12 weeks of continuous hypocaloric restriction to restore circulating leptin, thyroid T3, and alleviate psychological diet fatigue.
