# Hypertrophy Training Guidelines & Volume Landmarks

## 1. Scientific Volume Landmarks (Sets Per Muscle Group / Week)
Based on current meta-analyses and evidence-based training principles (Dr. Mike Israetel, Schoenfeld et al.):

| Muscle Group | Maintenance Volume (MV) | Minimum Effective Volume (MEV) | Maximum Adaptive Volume (MAV) | Maximum Recoverable Volume (MRV) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Chest** | 6-8 sets | 10-12 sets | 12-20 sets | 22+ sets |
| **Back (Lats & Traps)** | 8-10 sets | 12-14 sets | 14-22 sets | 25+ sets |
| **Quadriceps** | 6-8 sets | 10-12 sets | 12-18 sets | 20+ sets |
| **Hamstrings** | 4-6 sets | 8-10 sets | 10-16 sets | 18+ sets |
| **Glutes** | 0-4 sets | 6-8 sets | 8-14 sets | 16+ sets |
| **Side & Rear Delts** | 6-8 sets | 10-12 sets | 16-24 sets | 26+ sets |
| **Biceps** | 4-6 sets | 8-10 sets | 10-16 sets | 20+ sets |
| **Triceps** | 4-6 sets | 6-8 sets | 10-14 sets | 18+ sets |
| **Calves & Abs** | 0-6 sets | 8-10 sets | 12-16 sets | 20+ sets |

*Note: Sets must be taken between RPE 6 and RPE 10 to count toward effective hypertrophic volume.*

## 2. RPE to RIR (Reps in Reserve) Calibration Table
The Rate of Perceived Exertion (RPE) scale directly correlates to proximity to failure (RIR):

| RPE | Reps in Reserve (RIR) | Training Intent / Fatigue Profile |
|---|---|---|
| **10** | 0 RIR | Absolute concentric failure. No further reps possible with acceptable form. Reserved for final sets/microcycle peaks. |
| **9** | 1 RIR | Very high stimulus, minimal axial fatigue. Exactly 1 rep left in reserve. |
| **8** | 2 RIR | Optimal hypertrophic baseline for working sets. Balances stimulus-to-fatigue ratio (SFR). |
| **7** | 3 RIR | Moderate stimulus, ideal for technical compounds, warm-up work, or active recovery days. |
| **6** | 4 RIR | Minimum threshold for muscle hypertrophy. Appropriate for deloads or rapid movement speed focus. |
| **< 6** | 5+ RIR | Warm-up or conditioning only. Does not count toward hypertrophic weekly set volume. |

## 3. Progressive Overload Framework: Double Progression
When adjusting resistance and volume across mesocycles:
1. **Target Rep Bracket**: Assign a target rep range (e.g., 8-12 reps per set).
2. **Rep Accumulation**: Maintain the exact load until the trainee reaches the upper rep target (12 reps) across **all** designated working sets with valid RPE.
3. **Load Escalation**: Increase the weight by **2% to 5%** (micro-loading for isolation movements, 2.5-5kg for compound movements).
4. **Rep Reset**: Upon increasing weight, the reps naturally drop back toward the lower boundary (e.g., 8 reps). The cycle repeats.
5. **Deload Protocol**: Implement a scheduled deload every 4-6 weeks (reduce volume by 40-50% and intensity to RPE 6-7).
